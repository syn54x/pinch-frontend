Why it moved
Setup holds the surfaces you work in — Connections, Categories & Rules. Settings is account configuration you visit twice a year. Splitting them stops the nav from pretending those are the same kind of thing.
Kept deliberately
⌘, still opens Settings from anywhere, and every pane stays deep-linkable — burying a developer token two clicks deeper would be a real cost to the people who use it most.
Kept out
